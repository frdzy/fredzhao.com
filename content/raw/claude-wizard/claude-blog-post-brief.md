## Goal

A single blog post using the Wizarding Office as the sole mental model for Claude Code. Targeted at a technical-but-not-systems-engineer reader — someone who knows what Claude is and wants a better mental model for using it effectively.

---

## Key beats, roughly in order

Open with an intro: I was introduced the world of Harry Potter 25 years ago, and the world of
Claude Code 3 months ago. This weekend I thought about all their similarities. This blog is what
came out as a result, coauthored by me and my friend Gilderoy Lockhart

1. Introduce the Forgetful Wizard (Lockhart) — powerful, no memory between sessions, prone to confabulation
2. The Desk — fixed size, inexplicably can't be enlarged even with magic; attention is the bottleneck, not power; the dumb zone means synthesis errors with false confidence, not just slowness
3. Permanent papers above the desk (CLAUDE.md) — survive when the desk is cleared
4. The spell book (skills) — spine always visible, pages load on invocation
5. Summoned duplicates (subagents) — the underappreciated reason is desk hygiene, not speed
6. Goblin enchantments (hooks + scripts) — the only layer that actually enforces; three levels, the vault lock doesn't exist yet
7. Knowledge sharing — Sectumsempra vs. standard-issue spell book
8. Provider upgrades — Professor Academy race: better professors, but parchments keep growing

## Tone
